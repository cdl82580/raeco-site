About

Style meets connection.

At Rae+Co, we believe beauty is more than a look. It's a feeling. Our team of talented independent beauty professionals creates personalized experiences that leave every guest feeling confident, refreshed, and cared for.

From lived-in color and extensions to precision cuts and luxury treatments, we combine artistry with expertise to bring your vision to life. We are proud to be a diverse, inclusive, and multicultural salon where all guests feel comfortable, valued, and celebrated.

Our story. Established in 2009 as Textures Salon, Beth opened a two-chair salon in Clinton with a mission to provide high-quality services at reasonable prices. It has grown into a thriving 5-station salon with a nail technician and a luxurious spa room, filled with talented independent professionals and amazing guests, now called Rae+Co.

Our approach. Whether you've never been to a salon, you're starting a fresh new look, or reaching for a long-term goal, our commitment is to collaborate and move you toward it.
